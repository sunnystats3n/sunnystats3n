## Hi there 👋

A little Intro about me

I am deeply committed to uncovering the data quality problems that undermine trustworthy analytics and governed AI, and to building the controls that catch them before they reach a decision. My approach is methodologically flexible, yet I am inclined to employ data governance, data quality management, and statistical analysis techniques, complemented by essential regulatory practices, particularly in model risk governance, to build dependable, audit-ready data and AI systems. As a data professional focused on outcomes, I prioritize the most suitable framework or control over adherence to any single methodology or tool. My decision-making is guided by the specific regulatory and business requirements of the problem and the most defensible, evidence-based means of addressing them. Outside of actively building data governance and AI risk portfolio projects, I dedicate my time to statistical research with seven peer-reviewed publications and counting. I am a member of DAMA Toronto and ISDSA data science communities.
